We built NoiseProof for the Gnani AI Great Indian Internship Challenge.

It generates synthetic Hinglish phone calls, drowns them in 5 noise environments down to 0 dB SNR, and measures exactly what the AI hears.

Word Error Rate at clean speech: 5%.
Word Error Rate at 0 dB noise: 67%.

Key-fact recall holds above 80% down to 10 dB SNR.

Built with Gnani Prisma STT + Timbre TTS + Evon LLM.
