What happens when you throw an AI voice agent into 0 dB street noise in India? 🎧⚡

Indian telephonic audio is notoriously brutal for speech models: bustling sabzi mandis, honking traffic, crowd chatter, and 8 kHz ITU-T G.711 telephony compression. Most voice bots hallucinate or collapse completely.

For The Great Indian AI Internship Challenge by Gnani.ai, I built NoiseProof — an end-to-end benchmarking and call intelligence platform engineered specifically for the "Noise Canceller" award track (0 dB Tolerance).

Here is the experiment:
We generated realistic, code-mixed Hinglish customer-support calls, drowned them in 5 distinct Indian acoustic noise environments down to 0 dB Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR), and measured what actually survived.

The Architecture (powered by Gnani's Vachana Suite):
🎙️ Gnani Prisma v2.5 (STT): High-throughput code-mixed Hinglish transcription under heavy acoustic degradation.
🗣️ Gnani Timbre v2.5 (TTS): Multi-voice dialogue generation — featuring "Poorvi" (professional Hinglish agent) and "Vikrant" (conversational Hindi customer).
🧠 Gnani Evon v3.3 (Reasoning LLM): Dialogue script generation, structured entity extraction, and reasoning summarization.
🛡️ Failsafe Architecture: SHA-256 disk-backed caching + hard API credit ceiling protection to prevent duplicate billings.

The Hard Benchmark Numbers:
📉 Word Error Rate (WER) degrades from 5% (clean baseline) to 67% at 0 dB SNR.
💡 The Breakthrough Finding: Critical Key-Fact Recall (numerical entities like ₹ loan amounts, account IDs, payment due dates) stayed above 80% down to 10 dB SNR! 

Even when verbatim word recognition takes a hit, downstream conversational intelligence and action-item extraction can still salvage business transactions if architected with structured reasoning.

Designed with an editorial "Shopify Editions Winter 2026 (The RenAIssance Edition)" aesthetic featuring a dual A/B audio player with synchronized clean vs. degraded waveform listening and real-time transcript diffing.

Why do most voice AI agents fail in India?

It’s rarely the language model — it’s the physical acoustic environment. 

A customer calling from a busy auto-rickshaw, a railway platform, or a noisy market experiences Signal-to-Noise Ratios (SNR) dropping near 0 dB, compounded by telephone bandpass clipping (300–3400 Hz).

To address this, I built NoiseProof for Gnani.ai's Great Indian AI Internship Challenge.

NoiseProof is a benchmarking suite that stress-tests conversational AI under real-world Indian acoustic chaos:
1️⃣ Deterministic RMS SNR mixing across 5 noise profiles (Street, Crowd, Wind, White Noise, Market).
2️⃣ Synthetic Hinglish dialogue generation using Gnani Timbre v2.5.
3️⃣ Code-mixed speech decoding via Gnani Prisma v2.5.
4️⃣ Entity retention & call summarization powered by Gnani Evon v3.3.

The metric that matters most isn't just WER — it's Key-Fact Recall. If a customer says "mera EMI amount ₹5,000 hai", does the financial transaction survive the noise?
